How these numbers are calculated
  • Calories: Mifflin–St Jeor equation (Mifflin et al., American Journal of Clinical Nutrition, 1990), multiplied by your activity level.
  • Protein: International Society of Sports Nutrition (2017): 1.4–2.0 g per kg for people who exercise. Above a BMI of 25 it is based on the weight at BMI 25.
  • Macro split: US Institute of Medicine ranges: protein 10–35%, fat 20–35%, carbs 45–65% of calories.
  • BMI: World Health Organization categories (18.5 / 25 / 30).
  • Water: US National Academies (3.7 L men, 2.7 L women of total water; about 80% of it from drinks).
  • Keto: under 50 g of carbs a day.
